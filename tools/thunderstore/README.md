# Enter The Gungeon Online Co-Op Mod

Online co-op for Enter the Gungeon with independent cameras, and experimental 3 and 4 player. Choose any character.

Start the game, pause, and pick **PLAY ONLINE**. Everyone needs the same version of this mod.

**Other mods:** everyone needs the same mods and versions. When you join, the mod lists any mods that differ. Mods that change how floors are built may not work online: if your floor differs from the host's, you are returned to the Breach with a message.

Full guide, known limits and bug reports: https://github.com/Hero88go/GungeonOnlineCoOpMod
